# Improve a buyer-relevant page

| Buyer question | Useful page/section | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Which options fit? | Category/use case | Fit, limits, prerequisites, alternatives |
| How do A and B compare? | Balanced comparison | Current boundaries, dates, comparable plans |
| What will I pay? | Pricing/limits | Billing basis, minimums, fees, regional caveats |
| Does it work with my stack? | Integration documentation | Supported actions, prerequisites, limitations |
| Why believe this? | Original case study/test | Method, sample, baseline, dates, reproducibility |
| How do I claim the offer? | Public terms | Benefit, eligibility, expiry/timezone, exclusions, steps |

Answer near the relevant question with an ordinary heading and concise paragraph/table. Give a quoted passage enough context to retain qualifications. Avoid artificial fragments and near-duplicate pages for each wording.

Use evidence the business can supply: documentation, reproducible benchmarks, authorized customer examples, or transparent methodology. Mark missing facts; do not turn aspirations into capabilities.

## Fact block pattern

Adapt rather than filling irrelevant fields:

```markdown
## Is [product] suitable for [buyer/use case]?

[Product] supports [specific task] for [eligible users] with [verified capability].
It fits [conditions]; it is less suitable for [concrete limitation].

| Factor | Current fact | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | [billing basis and meaningful limits] | [public source] |
| Integration | [supported action, not just a logo] | [documentation] |
| Limitation | [purchase-relevant constraint] | [source] |

Checked: [date]. See [stable destination] for current terms.
```

For an offer, publish exact benefit, eligible audience/region, dates/timezone where relevant, exclusions, and claim steps at a stable destination a human can use. Never invent incentives or deadlines. Reconcile conflicting versions before publication.

## External opportunities

Inspect whether the cited passage actually covers the category and the brand belongs. Identify a factual gap or useful original contribution: outdated pricing, a documented integration, a missing use case, or a reproducible comparison. Prepare a sourced correction/submission through the site's normal process. Record editorial independence and commercial relationships.

Do not guarantee placement or assume citations can be purchased. Avoid fabricated reviews, impersonation, undisclosed endorsements, and mass comment spam. A draft contribution is useful when sending is outside scope.

## Prioritization example

A relevant owned pricing page cited in 6/20 unbranded answers, with four quoting an obsolete limit, presents a concrete accuracy intervention: correct the limit and conflicting sources. A roundup cited in 12/20 is an external opportunity only after checking relevance, editorial policy, and a truthful inclusion reason. An uncited use-case page with buyer fit remains a discovery experiment.

These are illustrative numbers, never an observed client baseline.
